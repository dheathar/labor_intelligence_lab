🧠 Labor Intelligence Virtual Lab

A research environment for labor market demand & supply analysis. The lab covers:

  • Datasets — 22+ sources from BLS, ESCO, Eurostat, ELSTAT, Cedefop, ILOSTAT
  • Papers — 15+ indexed research papers (2022–2026)
  • Methods — Skill extraction, forecasting, wage prediction, OJA intelligence
  • Active OJA crawling — 12 countries, 50+ job portals
  • Projects — EU-ALMPO, MicroIdea, Growth4Blue, TRAIN4BLUE

    Key themes, state-of-the-art, and open problems in labor market intelligence.

Cedefop/Eurostat OJA Web Intelligence Hub represents production-scale EU deployment. Foundation models enable wage gap estimation without traditional survey data. Graph neural networks model occupational transitions with high accuracy.
