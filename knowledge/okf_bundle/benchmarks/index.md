# Benchmarks

Evaluation benchmarks for skill extraction, occupation classification, and labor forecasting.

- [AI Jobs Forecasting Benchmark](ai-jobs-forecasting-benchmark.md) - Benchmarks LLM performance on predicting occupational demand changes driven.
- [LLM4Jobs Benchmark](llm4jobs-benchmark.md) - Benchmark for unsupervised occupation extraction and standardisation from.
- [Multilingual OJA Occupation Classification Benchmark](oja-classification-benchmark.md) - Benchmark for evaluating automatic classification of online job advertisements to ISCO-08.
- [Skill-XL](skill-xl.md) - Extended exhaustive benchmark with sentence-level skill annotations.
- [SkillSpan](skillspan.md) - Hard and soft skill extraction from English job postings.
