## Description

Benchmark for evaluating automatic classification of online job advertisements to ISCO-08
occupation codes and ESCO occupation URIs. Tests multilingual performance across European
languages including low-resource Balkan languages.

## Metrics

top1-accuracy, top3-accuracy, f1-macro, f1-weighted
