# LIVLAB Knowledge Bundle

An [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/tree/main/okf) v0.1 bundle.
Generated from `knowledge/` source files. 174 concepts.

- [Papers](papers/) - Research papers on labor market NLP, online job advertisement intelligence, skills taxonomy, and ALMP evaluation. (21 concepts)
- [Datasets](datasets/) - Labor market datasets across Greece, the Western Balkans, EU, and global scope. (17 concepts)
- [Methods](methods/) - NLP and ML methods applied to labor market intelligence. (13 concepts)
- [Applications](applications/) - Use-case applications and dashboards built on LIVLAB data and methods. (9 concepts)
- [Benchmarks](benchmarks/) - Evaluation benchmarks for skill extraction, occupation classification, and labor forecasting. (5 concepts)
- [Wiki](wiki/) - Karpathy-style first-principles articles serving as the lab's internal AI/NLP knowledge base. (7 concepts)
- [Projects](projects/) - Active research projects (EU-ALMPO, MicroIdea, Growth4Blue, TRAIN4BLUE). (4 concepts)
- [Portals](portals/) - OJA crawling portals monitored across 9 countries. (41 concepts)
- [Deliverables](deliverables/) - Formal project deliverables with due dates, leads, and access levels. (25 concepts)
- [Work Packages](work_packages/) - Project work packages with tasks, leads, and deliverable mappings. (9 concepts)
- [Roles](roles/) - User personas that consume LIVLAB visualizations and data. (5 concepts)
- [Occupations](occupations/) - ISCO-08 reference occupations used in demand/supply analysis. (18 concepts)
