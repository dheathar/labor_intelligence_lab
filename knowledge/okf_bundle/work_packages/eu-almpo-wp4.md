# WP4: Crawling and Analysis of Online Job Postings and Administrative Data


**Project:** EU-ALMPO


Develop a user-friendly Labour Market Analysis Tool that integrates OJA data
and administrative records to support evidence-based ALMP design.

**Lead:** UOP

**Duration:** M6–M36

## Tasks

- T4.2: Administrative data integration (LFS, PES registers, Eurostat)
- T4.3: Labour Market Analysis Tool development
- T4.4: Ethics compliance and data governance
