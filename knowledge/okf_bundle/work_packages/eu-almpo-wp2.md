# WP2: In-depth analysis of skill matching strategies and approaches


**Project:** EU-ALMPO


In-depth analysis of skill-matching strategies in partner countries through stakeholder
mapping, good practice identification, and community of practice workshops.

**Lead:** GUT

**Duration:** M3–M12

## Tasks

- T2.1: Mapping of stakeholders and skills eco-systems in 5 partner countries
- T2.2: Identification of existing and emerging good practices
- T2.3: Ten in-depth case studies (2 per country)
- T2.4: Community of practice workshop on AI-based matching tools
