## Abstract

Uses large pre-trained foundation models to estimate wage gaps across
occupations, industries, and demographic groups. Demonstrates that foundation
model representations encode labour market wage signals, enabling estimation
of wage disparities at scale without traditional survey data.

## Key findings

- Foundation model embeddings correlate strongly with wage levels
- Gender and race wage gaps recoverable from text representations
- Enables large-scale wage estimation without survey data
- {'New paradigm': 'labour market analysis via foundation model probing'}
