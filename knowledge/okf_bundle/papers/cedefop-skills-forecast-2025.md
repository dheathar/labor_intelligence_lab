## Abstract

Cedefop's 2025 edition of the European Skills Forecast, projecting employment by sector,
occupation, and qualification level to 2035 for all EU-27 countries. Uses Cambridge Econometrics'
E3ME macroeconomic model (Module 1) combined with occupation/qualification projection modules.
Captures the twin (digital + green) transition, COVID-19 recovery, and demographic shifts.

## Key findings

- {'ICT & Professional Services': 'strongest job growth through 2035'}
- {'Health & Social Work': '+3.2M jobs EU27 (demographic pressure)'}
- {'Renewable energy sector': 'rapid expansion driving new occupation demand'}
- {'Manufacturing': 'net decline (-1.45M) driven by automation and offshoring'}
- {'Agriculture': 'continued structural decline (-680K)'}
- {'Qualification polarisation': 'demand grows fastest at high skill levels AND some low-skill services; middle skill hollowing out'}
- Green and digital transitions together drive most of the occupational churn
- 591 ESCO skills labelled as green; green skills increasingly mainstreamed across sectors
