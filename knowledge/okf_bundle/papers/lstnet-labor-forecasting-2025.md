## Abstract

Applies LSTNet (Long- and Short-Term Time-series Network) to U.S. BLS labor
market data spanning March 2006 – December 2024. Combines convolutional and
recurrent layers to capture multi-scale temporal patterns in employment levels,
average weekly hours, earnings, unemployment, job openings, hires, and separations.

## Key findings

- Neural networks outperform autoregressive models at 1- and 6-month horizons
- Multi-scale architecture captures both short-term shocks and long-term trends
- BLS JOLTS + CPS combination gives best predictive power
