## Abstract

Presentation at the Cedefop/Eurostat "Harnessing Web Data for Next-Generation Skills Intelligence"
conference (Thessaloniki, May 2026). Covers UOP's approach to automatically classifying
multilingual online job postings to ESCO occupations across the LIVLAB country scope —
EU member states and Western Balkan accession countries. Demonstrates that state-of-the-art
multilingual NLP models (fine-tuned on ESCO) achieve competitive classification performance
across Greek, Italian, Spanish, and Western Balkan language variants.

## Key findings

- UOP multilingual OJA classification is at the research frontier recognised by Cedefop/Eurostat
- ESCO fine-tuning on multilingual corpora significantly outperforms zero-shot classification
- Cross-lingual transfer works for closely related languages (e.g. Serbian → Macedonian)
- Western Balkan OJA corpora can be mapped to ESCO without separate training data for each language

## LIVLAB relevance

This is LIVLAB's own UOP research presented at a top EU conference. The methodology is the
backbone of EU-ALMPO WP4 — the ESCO-classification pipeline for the 12-country OJA crawler.
Core deliverable for D4.1 and D4.2.
