## Abstract

Domain-specific pre-training of XLM-R using the ESCO taxonomy as training
signal. Enables multilingual skill extraction and occupational classification
across European languages. Uses ESCO hierarchical structure to enrich
representations through taxonomy-driven masked language modelling.

## Key findings

- ESCO taxonomy as training signal significantly improves domain performance
- Outperforms general multilingual models on job market tasks
- Enables cross-lingual transfer for low-resource EU languages
- Strong performance on SkillSpan and Skill-XL benchmarks
