## Abstract

Analyses early-stage transformation of the U.S. labor market by GPT-class
generative AI models. Finds approximately 80% of the U.S. workforce could have
10% or more of their tasks affected. Particularly strong exposure in professional
and clerical occupations. Chatbot adoption associated with occupational switching
into higher-wage roles.

## Key findings

- ~80% of US workforce could have 10%+ of tasks affected by GPT-class models
- {'Strongest exposure': 'professional and clerical occupations'}
- Chatbot adoption linked to occupational switching into higher-wage roles
- Effect concentrated in college-educated workers
