## Abstract

Maps the DigComp 2.2 digital competence framework (5 areas, 21 competences, 8 proficiency
levels) to ESCO skills, enabling consistent measurement of digital skill demand in OJAs.
Resolves the fragmentation between the EU's two main digital skills frameworks and enables
proficiency-level analysis of digital skills demand from job postings.

## Key findings

- {'Full crosswalk': 'DigComp 2.2 competences mapped to ESCO skill URIs'}
- Enables proficiency-level digital skill demand measurement in OJAs
- Basic digital literacy systematically understated in OJAs vs. actual job requirements
- Advanced ICT skills (programming, data, AI) well-captured by OJA text mining
- DigComp Area 5 (Problem-solving) has lowest OJA signal — abstract to express in job ads
- Digital skills demand growing in ALL occupation groups, not just ICT sector

## LIVLAB relevance

Essential reference for EU-ALMPO WP4 digital skills tracking. LIVLAB should implement
the DigComp→ESCO crosswalk in its NLP pipeline to enable digital skills proficiency analysis
across the 12-country OJA dataset — comparable to Cedefop's digitalness index.
