## Abstract

Adapts general LLMs for labour market skill extraction using knowledge
distillation. A large teacher LLM annotates job postings; a smaller student
model learns from these annotations. Achieves near-teacher performance at
much lower inference cost.

## Key findings

- Knowledge distillation bridges gap between general LLMs and domain specialists
- Student model achieves 95%+ of teacher performance at 10x lower cost
- Particularly effective for long-tail skill categories
