## Abstract

Demonstrates that ML methods outperform conventional approaches for labour
market forecasting during the COVID-19 pandemic. Traditional ARIMA and VAR
models failed during the structural break; tree-based and neural approaches
were more robust to distribution shift.

## Key findings

- ML methods significantly outperform traditional econometric forecasts during COVID
- Structural breaks render conventional models unreliable
- Ensemble methods most robust across diverse economic conditions
- High-frequency alternative data improves pandemic-era forecasts
