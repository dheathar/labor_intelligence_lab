## Abstract

Comprehensive survey of 1,000+ employers covering 14M workers across 22
industry clusters and 55 economies. Projects labor market transformation
through 2030 with focus on skill requirements, reskilling needs, and
technology adoption.

## Key findings

- 63% of employers identify skill gaps as biggest barrier to transformation
- 59 of 100 workers need training by 2030
- Skills requirements changed 25% since 2015; accelerating toward 65% by 2030
- {'Top growing roles': 'AI/ML specialists, data analysts, fintech engineers'}
