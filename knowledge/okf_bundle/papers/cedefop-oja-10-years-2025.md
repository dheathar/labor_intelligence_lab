## Abstract

A decade-spanning review of how online job advertisement (OJA) data has matured from basic
collection to sophisticated labour market intelligence. Covers the full OJA processing pipeline
(scraping → dedup → NLP → ESCO mapping), quality challenges, and lessons from running the
Eurostat Web Intelligence Hub at scale across 32 European countries.

## Key findings

- OJA data enables near real-time skills demand tracking not possible with traditional surveys
- {'Full pipeline': 'landscaping → scraping/crawling/API → deduplication → NLP extraction → ESCO classification'}
- OJAR (Online Job Advertisement Rate) = OJA flow / LFS employment, at 3-digit ISCO level
- IT roles systematically overrepresented; public sector, craft trades underrepresented
- Green and digital skills can be extracted via NLP on raw OJA text mapped to ESCO taxonomy
- 591 ESCO skills/knowledge concepts labelled as green (as of 2025)
- Quarterly releases covering 27 EU states + EFTA + UK
- Skills-OVATE delivers country × occupation × skill analytics at NUTS-2 regional level
