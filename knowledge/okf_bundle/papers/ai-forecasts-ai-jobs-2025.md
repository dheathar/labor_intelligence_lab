## Abstract

Benchmarks LLM performance on predicting labor market changes, specifically
occupational demand shifts driven by AI adoption. Evaluates multiple LLMs
against ground-truth labor statistics and expert forecasts.

## Key findings

- LLMs show systematic biases in labor market prediction
- AI-related job demand is consistently underestimated
- Prompting strategy significantly affects forecast accuracy
- Establishes baseline benchmark for future evaluation
