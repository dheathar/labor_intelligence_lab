## Abstract

Analyses AI-driven labour market transitions using graph neural networks on
evidence from 10,000 Egyptian jobs. Models occupational transition probabilities
as a graph problem where nodes are occupations and edges encode transition
feasibility given AI exposure and skill adjacency.

## Key findings

- GNNs effectively model occupational transition pathways
- AI exposure creates clusters of vulnerable and complementary occupations
- Emerging market transitions differ structurally from OECD patterns
- Graph structure reveals non-obvious transition pathways
