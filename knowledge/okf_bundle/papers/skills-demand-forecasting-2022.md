## Abstract

Directly forecasts skills demand using online job postings. Addresses the
skills gap between workers and employer demands using representation learning
over temporal skill co-occurrence signals. Recognised by Labor Market
Information Council as a crucial research direction.

## Key findings

- Temporal representation learning captures skill demand dynamics
- Online job postings are a reliable real-time signal for skills demand
- Skill co-occurrence graph embeddings improve forecasting accuracy
- {'Risk': 'millions of unfilled positions without skilled workers'}
