## Abstract

Comprehensive survey consolidating methodologies, datasets, and terminologies
for deep learning approaches to job market analysis. Covers skill extraction,
skill classification, occupational coding, and benchmarking. Organises the
field into task types, dataset characteristics, and model families.

## Key findings

- Consolidates fragmented literature into unified taxonomy
- Identifies SkillSpan and Skill-XL as primary benchmarks
- LLM-based approaches now competitive with supervised baselines
- Multilingual coverage still a significant gap
