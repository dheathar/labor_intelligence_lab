## Abstract

IMF analysis of skill gaps created by AI adoption and pathways to bridge them
through new job creation. Examines which occupations are complemented vs
substituted by AI, and the policy interventions needed to support transitions.

## Key findings

- AI creates new job categories even as it automates existing tasks
- Complementary skills (creativity, social intelligence) most protected
- Policy interventions must target retraining speed and breadth
- Developing economies face asymmetric transition risks
