# Growth4Blue


_Growth4Blue — Blue Economy Skills Intelligence_


Interreg IPA ADRION project focusing on blue economy career pathways and
skills intelligence in the Adriatic-Ionian region. Combines maritime/coastal
job posting data with skills gap analysis for the blue economy sector.

## Countries

Greece, Italy, Croatia, Albania, Montenegro

## Data sources

- Marineria.it (maritime job portal)
- Adzuna Italy (coastal region filter)
- DYPA/Ergani (Greece)
- Eurostat NUTS2 coastal regions
