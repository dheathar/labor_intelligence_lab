## Description

The Greek Ministry's Labour Market Analysis tool (mdaae.gr) is the national
LMO portal for Greece. It provides integrated labor market statistics drawn
from DYPA (formerly OAED), ERGANI (employer declarations), and ELSTAT LFS.
Key outputs: monthly registered unemployment, vacancy stats, sector-level
employment trends, and ALMP participation tracking.
