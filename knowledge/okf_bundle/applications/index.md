- [OJA Skills Intelligence Dashboard](oja-intelligence-dashboard.md) - Online Job Advertisement (OJA)-based real-time dashboard for skills demand.
- [Online Job Advertisement (OJA) Portals Catalog](oja-portals-catalog.md) - Catalog of key Online Job Advertisement portals by country/region.
- [Regional Skills Ecosystem Mapping](regional-skills-ecosystem.md) - Framework for mapping regional labour market skills ecosystems by combining OJA demand signals.
- [Skills Gap Dashboard](skills-gap-dashboard.md) - Real-time dashboard layering job posting skill demand against workforce.
