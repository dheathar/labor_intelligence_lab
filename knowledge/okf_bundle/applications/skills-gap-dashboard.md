## Description

Real-time dashboard layering job posting skill demand against workforce
supply signals. Identifies skill shortages by occupation, sector, and
geography. Enables HR leaders and policymakers to spot emerging bottlenecks
before they become critical.

## Example tools

Cedefop Skills-OVATE, LinkedIn Talent Insights, Lightcast
