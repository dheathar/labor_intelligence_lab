## Description

Application for tracking green and blue economy skill demand through OJA time series.
Uses ESCO's 591 green-labelled skills + NLP lexicon expansion to compute sector-specific
greenness indices. Critical for TRAIN4BLUE and Growth4Blue blue economy skill mapping
in the Adriatic-Ionian region.
