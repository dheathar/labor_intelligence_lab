## Description

Unsupervised occupation extraction and standardisation using LLMs. Extracts
occupations from free-text job postings and resumes, then maps to standard
taxonomies (O*NET, ISCO) without requiring labeled training data. Essential
for job recommendation and labor market policy analysis.

## Strengths

- No labeled data required
- Handles novel job titles
- Generalizes across taxonomies

## Weaknesses

- LLM inference cost
- Less consistent on low-resource languages
