## Description

Models occupational transition probabilities as a graph problem where nodes
are occupations and edges encode transition feasibility based on skill
adjacency and AI exposure scores. GNNs learn complex non-linear relationships
between occupations that are invisible in simple feature matrices.

## Strengths

- Captures non-obvious transition pathways
- Incorporates skill adjacency structure
- Works for emerging market contexts

## Weaknesses

- Requires occupation graph construction
- Less interpretable than linear models
