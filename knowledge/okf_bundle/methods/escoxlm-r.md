## Description

Multilingual domain-specific pre-training of XLM-R using ESCO taxonomy as training
signal. Achieves state-of-the-art on multilingual skill extraction and occupational
classification. Best for EU multi-language job posting analysis.

## Strengths

- Multilingual (27 EU languages)
- Domain-adapted to labor market
- Strong zero-shot cross-lingual transfer

## Weaknesses

- Requires ESCO taxonomy alignment
- Not updated post-ESCO v1.2
