## Description

Uses large pre-trained foundation model embeddings to estimate wage levels
and disparities. Probes foundation model representations for labor market
signals without task-specific training. Enables large-scale wage gap analysis
across occupations, industries, and demographic groups.

## Strengths

- No labeled wage data required for many analyses
- Scalable across millions of job postings
- Captures implicit wage signals in text

## Weaknesses

- Foundation models may encode historical biases
- Probing accuracy degrades for niche occupations
