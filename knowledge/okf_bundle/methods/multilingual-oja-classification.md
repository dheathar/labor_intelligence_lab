## Description

Fine-tuning multilingual transformer models (mBERT, XLM-R, ESCOXLM-R) on ESCO-labelled
OJA corpora to automatically classify job postings to ISCO-08 occupations and ESCO skills
across multiple languages. UOP's primary NLP pipeline for the multi-country LIVLAB crawler.
