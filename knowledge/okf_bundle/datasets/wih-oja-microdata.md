## Description

The primary European OJA microdata infrastructure, operated by Eurostat's Web Intelligence
Hub. Collects hundreds of millions of job advertisements per year from thousands of sources
(job portals, PES websites, recruitment agencies, corporate sites) across 32 countries.
Accessible to ESS members and authorised researchers via DataLab (R/Python/SQL environment)
or Microdata Access Portal. WIH OJA microdata cannot be downloaded — analysis must run
within DataLab.
