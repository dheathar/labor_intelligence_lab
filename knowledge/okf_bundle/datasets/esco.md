## Description

EU's multilingual classification of occupations, skills, competences and
qualifications. Available in 27 languages. Used as gold-standard taxonomy
in most EU labor market NLP research (ESCOXLM-R, NNOSE, ESCOX, etc.).
Licensed under EUPL 1.2. Local and web-service API available.
Cross-walks to ISCO-08, O*NET, and national classifications.

## Key variables

- ESCO occupation URIs and preferred/alternative labels (27 languages)
- skill/competence URIs, labels, descriptions
- ISCO-08 crosswalk
- qualification types and EQF levels
- skill type (knowledge / skill / attitude)
