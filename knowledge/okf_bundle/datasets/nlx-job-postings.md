## Description

Largest U.S. job posting dataset available for academic research. Aggregates
postings from thousands of job boards and employer websites. Data points include
skills, credentials, pay, occupation, geography, and employer. Available to
qualified researchers via application. Used in skills demand forecasting research
(arXiv:2205.09508).

## Key variables

- job title and description text
- extracted skills (normalized)
- occupation codes (SOC)
- compensation (when stated)
- employer and location
- posting duration and close date
