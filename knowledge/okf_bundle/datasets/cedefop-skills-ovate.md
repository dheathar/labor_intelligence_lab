## Description

Cedefop's flagship labour market intelligence tool built on WIH OJA microdata. Delivers
near real-time skills and occupation demand analytics for 32 European countries. The
primary public-facing interface for Cedefop's OJA intelligence at occupation × skill ×
country × region × sector granularity.
