## Description

The only synchronized European employment forecast by occupation and sector.
Produced by Cedefop using EUKLEMS, Eurostat LFS, and expert inputs. Projects
employment demand at sector × occupation level through 2035. Available as
downloadable spreadsheets and via interactive Skillsnet portal.
Complementary to Cedefop STAS (short-term) and Skills Shortage Index.

## Key variables

- employment level by occupation × sector (42 sectors, 9 groups)
- employment growth projections 2025–2035
- skills requirements by occupation
- VET participation and outcomes
