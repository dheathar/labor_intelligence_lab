## Description

Worker-level survey on skill requirements, skills utilisation, digital technology
impacts, and skills mismatch. Wave 2 (2021) microdata now available for research.
Captures how AI and digital tools are changing work tasks and skill demands from
the workers' perspective (complementary to employer-side OJA data).

## Key variables

- skill requirements by task type
- digital technology usage and impact
- skills mismatch (over/under-qualification)
- training participation
- work changes due to automation
