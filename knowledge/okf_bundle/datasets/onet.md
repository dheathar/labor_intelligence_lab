## Description

The primary U.S. occupational taxonomy. ~1,000 occupations described across
270+ standardized descriptors: knowledge, skills, abilities, work activities,
work context, interests, work values, and education/training requirements.
Free API access. Foundation for most U.S. labor market analysis.
Cross-walked to ESCO for international comparisons.

## Key variables

- O*NET-SOC occupation codes and titles
- 35 skills (communication, critical thinking, etc.)
- 41 knowledge areas
- 52 work activities
- education/training requirements
- median wages (linked to BLS OES)
