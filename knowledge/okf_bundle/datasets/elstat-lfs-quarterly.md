## Description

Quarterly Greek LFS following ILO/Eurostat methodology. Published as Excel
downloads by ELSTAT. Covers employment status, economic activity (NACE),
occupational data, working time, underemployment and unemployment duration.
National + regional (NUTS2) + urban/rural breakdowns.
Contact: k.loizos@statistics.gr for custom data requests.

## Key variables

- employment by sector (NACE), occupation, age, sex, education, nationality
- unemployment duration and characteristics
- potential labour force and underemployment
- urban/rural classification
- NUTS2 regional breakdown
