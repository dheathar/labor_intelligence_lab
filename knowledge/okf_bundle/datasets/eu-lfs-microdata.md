## Description

Individual-level household survey data — the largest European labour survey.
Covers 27 EU member states + Iceland, Norway, Switzerland, UK (to Q3 2020).
Access requires two-step registration: (1) organisation recognition as research
entity (~4 weeks), (2) research proposal via Microdata Access Portal (~8-10 weeks).
Variables anonymized/partially anonymized. Quarterly data enables employment
transitions analysis over 40+ years.

## Key variables

- age (5-year bands), citizenship, country of birth
- occupation (ISCO 3-digit)
- industry (NACE 1-digit)
- employment status, working time (full/part)
- NUTS Level 2 region
- income (deciles)
- education level
