## Description

The longest continuous Greek labour market series — 44 years from 1981.
Annual LFS aggregates covering population demographics, education levels,
employment and unemployment. Three Excel files: Population/Education/Employment
Status; Employed persons; Unemployed persons.
Contact: lfs@statistics.gr for data access.

## Key variables

- population and education demographics
- employment and unemployment levels (national)
- long historical time series back to 1981
