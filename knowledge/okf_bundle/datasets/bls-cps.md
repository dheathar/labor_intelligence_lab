## Description

The flagship U.S. household survey. Covers employment status, occupation,
industry, earnings, hours worked, and demographic characteristics for the
civilian non-institutional population 16+. Jointly produced with the Census Bureau.
CPS microdata harmonized through IPUMS for longitudinal analysis.

## Key variables

- employment status (employed/unemployed/not in labor force)
- occupation (SOC codes)
- industry (NAICS codes)
- usual weekly earnings
- hours worked
- education attainment
- age, sex, race, ethnicity
