## Description

Harmonized official EU labour market statistics from all member states.
Includes LFS-derived aggregates, job vacancy statistics (NACE Rev.2 quarterly),
labour costs and cost indices, earnings (gross/net, gender pay gaps, minimum wages),
and NUTS2-level regional breakdowns. REST API and SDMX 3.0 + 2.1 APIs available.
Updated twice daily.

## Key variables

- employment rates (total, by age, sex, education, NUTS2)
- unemployment rates (overall, youth, long-term)
- job vacancy rate (by sector)
- gender pay gap
- labour cost index
- minimum wage levels
