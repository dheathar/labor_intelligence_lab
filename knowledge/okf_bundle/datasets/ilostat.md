## Description

World's largest labour statistics repository. Free and open access with
multiple access modes: online viewer, Excel add-in, bulk download (CSV + dictionary),
Rilostat R package, and SDMX web service APIs. Greece country profile available
with 2024–2025 Q2 data. Critical for global and cross-country comparative analysis.

## Key variables

- employment and unemployment (by sex, age, education, sector)
- wages and earnings
- working time and hours
- labour productivity
- informal employment
- labour force participation
