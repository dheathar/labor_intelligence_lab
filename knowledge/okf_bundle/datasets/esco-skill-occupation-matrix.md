## Description

Downloadable matrix linking ISCO-08 occupation groups to ESCO skill hierarchical groups.
Shows the share of ESCO skills within each occupation group at multiple ISCO hierarchy levels
(1–4 digit) and ESCO skill levels (1–3). Enables visual heatmaps of occupation × skill
relationships without running full NLP on OJAs.
